Get set up

Discovery-first outreach. Sourcing is fixed to Canonical; drafts are never auto-sent — you send by hand.

    1 · Agent

    coldtrail drives an agent. Use a headless CLI (Claude Code / Codex) or bring your own model.

    Any OpenAI-compatible endpoint — local Ollama or a BYOK gateway. Note: sourcing (Canonical) & sending (Gmail) need the Claude/Codex backend for now.

    2 · Discovery

    Where companies come from. Canonical — verified, long-tail company search. Connecting authorizes it for your chosen provider (in-browser for BYOK/Ollama).

    3 · Destination

    Where outreach goes. Gmail — you review every draft and hit Send; coldtrail never auto-sends.

    ✓ Gmail is provided by your account's Gmail connector — already available on Claude Code / Codex, no keys to enter. Manage it at claude.ai → Connectors.

    One browser consent, no keys — coldtrail brings its own Google client. You'll pass Google's "unverified app" screen (Advanced → continue).

    also a destination: LinkedIn — coming

    4 · Your brief

    Your outreach brief (message.toml) — your voice, offer, and link. The agent writes a personalized pitch per company from it; it never sends this verbatim. Stays local.

    5 · Already contacted

    Domains you've reached before (contacted.toml) so coldtrail never double-contacts.
